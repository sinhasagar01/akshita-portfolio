⚠ Three things the implementation must derive rather than copy.

1 · The shadow's dark answer. #268's overlay scale is cream-derived and a fixed white or a fixed ink reads differently on a near-black pane. Derive the floating tier against the dark ground as the nav's was, and do not reuse the nav's alpha — pane luminance and target separation are two variables and the panes differ.

2 · The chip's tint on nine grounds. color-mix against the accent keeps it relational, but verify the chip glyph clears 4.5 on its own fill on all nine palettes, and the fill clears its ground. This is the only tinted surface in the toast, deliberately.

3 · The action link. accent-text measured 7.22 / 6.87 / 6.25 on cream-50/100/200 and is uncovered by the contrast gate — hazard 30. Measure it on the toast's ground on all nine rather than assuming those figures transfer.

⚠ Decided here unless overruled. Pending morphs into the result in place rather than being replaced, so a two-phase action reads as one object. Refusals and failures never auto-dismiss, because they name a post and a rule and the author has to act on them. Past the cap the oldest is dismissed rather than queued, so nothing appears after the author has stopped looking.
