The process

Measure first, then draw. A rating of 2.3 is a completion number wearing a satisfaction number.

Nothing is ever in the same place at the same time, so it needs no collision rule at all — which is the strongest thing about it, given this site has spent two PRs on floating furniture colliding. Cost: a control that is absent when you first look is a control most visitors never learn exists, and the hero is where their attention is.

Mobile — 390px

The hero stacks: copy above, artwork below, pieces hidden, scroll cue gone. The pill's corner is the thumb's home position and Safari's toolbar occupies that edge and animates as you scroll, so it is replaced rather than shrunk. The rail takes the right edge, is present from the first frame, and touches no corner.

Out of the thumb arc on purpose — discoverable without being pressed by accident, and it never touches the bottom edge the OS owns and animates.

Vertical is what makes it fit. Four dots and an arrow need 19px of width, and the right edge at mid-height is the one region this hero leaves empty at every phone size.

And it is present from the first frame, unlike the pill. The desktop reveal exists because the scroll cue owns that corner; on a phone the cue is gone, so there is nothing to wait for. Two placements, two reveal rules, and both follow from what else is on screen.

⚠ The honest cost, stated: on 390px the palette IS the whole screen. There is no side-by-side, so a press is not a demonstration — it is a differently coloured site. The rail earns its place by routing to /palettes, not by the switching.

⚠ Three things that decide this more than placement does. A press here is Try across portfolio, so it writes the same cookie and raises the same indicator — the one at the bottom of this page. Two mechanisms for one state is how an exit action stops working. Whether the four include the published theme, or a visitor's first press is a departure from a state they never saw. And whether the teaser survives navigation: you said homepage only, but a control that vanishes when you click Work is a control that appears to break — the indicator has to persist even if the dots do not.
