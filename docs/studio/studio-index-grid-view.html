⚠ Every row inside the card is a grid with explicit tracks

The first attempt used nested flex and the reorder cluster stretched to fill its row. Adding flex: 0 0 auto at each level did not fix it, because the fragility was the structure rather than any one declaration. The card's foot is now grid-template-columns: 1fr auto — the meta takes the flexible track and the cluster is its own fixed 52px box. Nothing negotiates.

The card is elevated, not bordered

Cream-50 cards on a cream-100 grid, because a card on the same ground has nothing to lift off. That is the Board's language exactly, so the two surfaces agree, and it checks out against the ladder — a field surface holding cards is what cream-100 is for. ⚠ The elevation is three role-named steps, not three literals. #168 recorded the shadow literal as an exception because the theme has no elevation scale; three steps across a grid is a scale arriving undeclared, and it gets declared beside the radius block or the next surface copies a literal.

← / → , and why

A grid wraps, so the study before a row's first card is visually to its left. ▲ would move a card to the end of the previous row, which reads as a jump rather than a step. The first card's ← and the last card's → are disabled rather than absent, so the control never moves between cards.

Two defects this fixes

boAt Crest reads as disabled today — dimmed because it is bespoke, but bespoke is not disabled: title, hero, summary and platform are all editable and only the body is in code. Hazard 29's shape one screen earlier; here it is a chip at full text strength. And "0 sections" becomes "No sections" — a zero beside three fifteens reads as a study that lost its content.

The summary reserves its two lines

Two lines is the budget and the box reserves the height whether or not the text fills it — otherwise a short summary shortens its card and the grid's rows stop aligning. Measure the longest real summary before accepting the clamp.

The card cannot be a button element

It is clickable and it contains buttons. A button inside a button is an invalid content model — #176's finding on a new surface. So it is a div with role="button" and tabindex, stopPropagation on the inner controls, and an authored focus ring, since a role-button div gets no ring and no Space/Enter for free.
