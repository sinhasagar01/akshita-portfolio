The four tiers

T0 · the reveal

420ms · conditional · scroll

Fires only if the target is out of view, on either pane. The mark starts at 55% of the scroll rather than after it — overlapped, not queued, so it never feels sequenced.

T1 · the lead

220ms · 0ms · glide

The accent bar wipes top-down — the only thing that draws itself rather than fading. It answers "what did I click", so it answers first.

T2 · the structure

340ms · 40ms · spring

The dock rises 20px with ~3px of overshoot. Slower and larger because it is a bigger object — a big thing moving at a small thing's speed reads as weightless.

T3 · the echo

260ms · 90ms · glide

The field's mark fades in place, no travel, deliberately understated. If it competed with T1 the eye would not know which was the answer.

T4 · the details

280ms · 150 / 190ms · glide

The tag, then the field, 6px each. The dock arrives and then fills rather than appearing whole.

Only one element springs

--ease-glide for everything that arrives; --ease-spring for the dock alone, because it is the only thing travelling far enough for a settle to read. A 6px detail on a spring is a wobble; a 20px panel on a spring is weight.

Dismiss is 40% of the time and collapsed

130ms against 340, everything at once, no stagger, no overshoot. An exit that mirrors its entrance makes dismissing feel like undoing.

What is new and must be declared

The studio has --duration-fast 150ms and --ease-out-expo, and nothing else. Two easings, five durations, four delays and two distances are additions — they belong in globals.css beside the radius scale with their tiers named, or the next person adding motion picks by copy-paste. Reduced motion zeroes every one and the final state is pixel-identical.
