The setup

One companion,
three very different people.

Enterprise AI isn't difficult because of the technology — it's difficult because every user expects something different. Inside Fosfor Decision Cloud, one AI companion had to support three distinct personas with different goals, workflows and definitions of success.

Designing one experience that felt intelligent to all of them became the core challenge of this project, and the thing every later decision had to answer to.

The challenge wasn't designing one AI assistant. It was designing one assistant that could think differently for three completely different people without making any of them feel like the product wasn't built for them.

Each persona solved different problems, which made a single one-size-fits-all experience ineffective from the first week of research onward.

Ink and cream are one rule

One step of separation from the surface the bar sits on. A cream thumb on ink and an ink thumb on cream both satisfy it — a single colour for both is what makes the sidebar look broken today. The ninth by-role answer in this project.

Firefox is a stated difference

It offers only scrollbar-width and scrollbar-color — no radius, no hover, no track control, and thin is ~11px rather than 6. The colours still apply, which is the half that matters for the ink sidebar. Measure what it renders rather than assuming it degrades cleanly.

The gutter is not touched, and that is why this variant won

globals.css:222 sets scrollbar-gutter: stable on html. matchMedia matches the viewport while the layout receives the viewport minus the reserved gutter, so every studio threshold inherits a ~15px discrepancy — 0 with overlay scrollbars, ~17 on Windows. #235 fixed the comparison rather than the gutter, deliberately. The overlay variant would have reclaimed 15px on every pane and required re-deriving every threshold to do it. Confirm the 6px width does not change the reserved gutter before building.

The surfaces — and a census from source is the first step

sidebar (ink-950, the one actually broken), list rail (cream-200), canvas (cream-50), inspector (cream-100), every textarea (cream-50), and the document, which owns the reserved gutter. This file is not evidence — a surface missed is a default scrollbar left on screen.

The textarea is a different case from a pane

It is small and its thumb is short. 6px on an 86px box may be a target nobody can hit — drawn here at the same 6px, which is the proposal rather than the finding. Measure the rendered thumb height on the smallest textarea in the studio and say whether it needs its own step.
