A's shape with B's frames: the palettes-page hero, then the fanned strip, then the honest fact row. Press the toggle — the same hero is shown with items and with none, because the empty case is what ships on day one and it must not be a different design.

⚠ The strip and the facts are both derived, and that is the whole point of showing two states. The frames are the five most recent items and the counts are real; with nothing in the collection the strip becomes five dashed placeholders and every count reads zero. Nothing is invented in either state — the date chip disappears rather than guessing, because no item carries a year yet.

The strip sits between the actions and the facts, so the hero still reads top-down as claim → invitation → evidence, and the frames straighten on hover — one gesture that says they are pieces rather than decoration.

⚠ The empty state is the one that ships today and it is deliberately the same design. Five dashed placeholders where the frames go, four greyed zeros, no date chip, filters present but disabled, and a sentence that says the gallery is empty rather than that a category is. A hero that changes shape on the first upload is two designs, and the record already holds one page that grew a second layout that way.

⚠ And the filtered-vs-empty distinction is fixed here. Your screenshot showed "Nothing here yet in that category" on a gallery with no items at all — the filtered message answering the unfiltered question. Two zero states, two sentences: Nothing here yet when the collection is empty, No drawings yet when a filter has none.

⚠ Two things this cannot settle. The date chip needs an item carrying a year, so it is absent until one does — deriving a range from nothing is the invention this record keeps deleting. And "See the work instead" sends a visitor away; that is right if the gallery is a footnote and wrong if it is a draw, and it is a positioning call rather than a design one.
